The complete v5 trial did not justify further label expansion for the current
outcome-GBM hypothesis. All1536 assigned fresh requests and one warmup completed.
Selection used only512 fit questions; the unchanged analysis then evaluated the
locked rules on256 disjoint questions. The primary GBM chose ten on247/256 cases,
matched fixed-ten's66 strict successes, lost one answer match (181 versus182),
and saved only2.13% reconstructed R. The registered targets were20% lower R,
0.25s lower mean T and no point S/E deficit against both fit-selected B and ten.
All conditions failed against at least one anchor. The selected proxy comparator
was faster with66 strict successes and182 answer matches. This is a failed
bounded development screen, not a population result or proof that allocation
cannot work.

The independently established [v3 reader admission](../allocation-continuous-v3/report.md)
remains valid: the same-model, same-ten-paragraph paired comparison produced
103/512 strict successes versus27/512 and344/512 answer matches versus260/512,

allocation result. The [v4 oracle screen](../allocation-action-v4/report.md)
identified a useful optimistic action frontier; v5 shows why privileged action
value does not establish learnable policy value.

## Research disposition

Stop allocation-label expansion under this registered hypothesis and retain
fixed-ten with the admitted recipe as the research anchor. The cheap42-feature
scalar learner has not established a useful allocation advantage at these fit
sizes. Simpler controls deserve an honest description, not a GBM-specific claim.
This result does not isolate whether signal, labels, threshold selection,
sampling precision or action design is the dominant limitation.

The next justified work is a bounded, method-blinded review of held-out/development
rescue/harm mechanisms, with qualified independent humans resolving important
semantic/reference disagreements. Any new learner target or intervention needs
a diagnosed mechanism, separate hypothesis/protocol and new independent data.
Do not retune this holdout, reopen prior enlarged-reader/selector screens or
append cases until a boundary passes. A future benchmark allocation claim still
requires the unchanged simultaneous zero-margin quality guards, frozen common
A*/B* and direct eligible R interval wholly below zero.

Human semantic adjudication, controlled host suspension and full original A03
raw/source authentication remain unperformed. Operational service-quality floor,
worthwhile saving, latency/failure limits, representative workload and deployment
horizon are unknown. No production or commercial benefit is demonstrated.
