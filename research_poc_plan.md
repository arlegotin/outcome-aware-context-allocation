## Assessment to Preserve

Select the narrowly defined context-allocation component of Outcome-Aware Search. Its candidate predicts the observed difference in joint-EM success between two and ten paragraphs, without paying for a draft first. Its primary objective is complete warm seconds per benchmark-supported success against one comparator selected on disjoint tuning data. Adaptation cost, quality, failures, latency tails and transfer are separate evidence requirements.

The supplied research assessment identifies substantial overlap with recoverability routing and learned evidence control. Adaptive-k is a mandatory cheap comparator; fixed intermediate budgets, ridge/RF, query-only, proxy-label, post-answer outcome and paid sufficiency controls are required. Do not claim a new scientific mechanism, a full reproduction of those papers, universal superiority or a commercial result.

The other proposed business directions depend on causal evidence unavailable from a one-laptop experiment: paid adoption, permissioned contribution quality, recurring maintenance value, historical coverage or publisher supply. Evaluation discipline can be reused without claiming those businesses have been tested.

## Remaining Limits and Decision Discipline

The public benchmark may be contaminated, its support annotations can be incomplete, strict joint EM may be insensitive with this reader, and a 24-hour sample may not resolve small quality differences. Timing nonstationarity, cache parity, total adaptation cost and corpus transfer can overturn an apparently favorable result. The population inference is finite-benchmark inference, not a future-customer guarantee.

Preserve failures and unfavorable comparisons. A tuned simpler method, informative lack of headroom, narrow positive operating point, unresolved precision or prerequisite block can each be useful evidence. Further research, a different action/model/metric or customer-provider study must be recorded as a new or amended experiment, never silently used to rescue the primary result.
