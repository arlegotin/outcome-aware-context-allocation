This prospectively replaces Gate 1's tentative suggestion of comparing 48 new 14B calls with historical 4B outputs. No historical per-question correctness is used for selection or comparison.

Development admits the fresh stage only if execution is healthy and complete, the 14B natural-ten joint-EM count is strictly greater than the 4B count, and its answer-EM counts are no lower at both natural budgets. Abstentions and malformed/truncated outputs remain failed outcomes, not grounds for replacing questions. Infrastructure/timeouts/context overflow or unknown quiescence block complete-stage admission. This conservative empirical screen can reject a modest benefit; it is not a significance test or proof of preserved quality.

Before these outcomes, reserve an independent, fixed **128-question** fresh sample. If admitted, run both readers at natural two/ten: **512 assigned calls** plus warmups. No interim quality peeking, sample extensions, model/prompt/metric changes, or new admission rule after outcomes.
